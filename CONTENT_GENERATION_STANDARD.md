# Orion Effects — Content Generation Standard

This file is the operating instruction for generating new Orion Effects social content and GitHub review issues.

It should be used together with `CONTENT_ACQUISITION_STRATEGY.md`.

## 1. Commercial objective

Every post must support this path:

**Reach → Recognition → Conversation → Qualified Opportunity → Client**

Do not generate content merely to fill a calendar, maximise impressions or repeat common marketing advice.

## 2. Classify the post before writing

Choose exactly one primary purpose:

- **Authority** — demonstrate experienced commercial and advertising judgement.
- **Conversation** — create a useful discussion with founders and business decision-makers.
- **Conversion** — give a qualified prospect a specific reason to start a conversation with Orion Effects.

Also define the target decision-maker, for example Founder, CEO, Marketing Director, Growth Lead or Commercial Director.

## 3. Writing approach

Prefer first-person operator language for LinkedIn when appropriate, especially from Gurmohan Singh's perspective.

Good patterns:

- `I would rather...`
- `One of the first things I would check...`
- `I would not increase this budget yet.`
- `What would make me scale this campaign?`
- `If this account came to us, this is where I would start.`

Do not impersonate experience that has not happened. Never fabricate clients, case studies, conversations, revenue, ROAS, CPL, sales outcomes or other results.

If using hypothetical numbers or situations, explicitly label them **illustrative scenario**.

## 4. Default post structure

Use this structure for approximately 80% of posts:

1. **Situation** — a realistic business problem.
2. **Observation** — what stands out and why it matters.
3. **Investigation** — what Orion Effects would check first.
4. **Decision** — what we would do, not do, stop, test or scale.
5. **Commercial connection** — connect advertising to pipeline, customers, margin or revenue.
6. **Ending** — insight, discussion question or selective conversion CTA.

Do not default to `5 tips`, `3 hacks`, definitions or generic teaching posts.

## 5. Hooks

The hook should contain tension, a decision or a business consequence.

Prefer:

`I would rather tell a client not to increase their Meta budget than scale an offer I don't believe is ready.`

Over:

`5 ways to improve your Meta Ads.`

Prefer:

`The CPL went down. I still wouldn't scale it.`

Over:

`Why lead quality matters.`

Hooks must remain truthful and should not imply a real client case unless one exists.

## 6. Business metrics

Do not evaluate success only through platform metrics.

Connect:

**Spend → Qualified Leads → Conversations → Opportunities → Customers → Revenue**

CTR, CPC, CPM, CPL, frequency and ROAS may be discussed, but only with the business context required to interpret them.

## 7. CTA selection

Choose one CTA mode and record it in the issue.

### Insight
No hard CTA. End with the strongest commercial lesson.

### Discussion
Ask a genuine question relevant to the decision-maker. Avoid engagement bait.

### Specific action
Use selectively for Conversion posts. Give the prospect a defined reason to respond.

Examples:

`If your Meta leads look good in Ads Manager but sales disagrees, message me PIPELINE. I'll share the first three places I would investigate.`

`Comment AUDIT and I'll share the framework we use to decide whether a campaign deserves more budget.`

Avoid generic `contact us`, website CTAs, support-desk language and promises to fix platform enforcement issues.

## 8. Orion Effects positioning

Services:

**Meta Ads • Lead Generation • Creative Content • Campaign Strategy**

The content should communicate the higher-level value:

**Better advertising and commercial decisions.**

Show how Orion Effects decides:

**What deserves budget → What should be tested → What should stop → What earns the right to scale**

Orion Effects is AI-powered but judgement-led. AI improves speed, research, analysis, creative production and workflow efficiency. Human commercial judgement remains responsible for market, offer, budget, qualification and scale decisions.

## 9. Image standard

Default to one static 4:5 image unless video is explicitly requested.

The GitHub issue must contain:

- **Image key text** — normally the hook or its strongest shortened form.
- **Supporting line** — optional and brief.
- **Creative direction** — a concrete visual concept tied to the business decision.

Brand:

- real Orion Effects logo, unchanged
- deep navy
- warm gold
- restrained cream
- premium, realistic, tactile editorial treatment

Avoid:

- robots
- holograms
- fake Ads Manager screens
- fake statistics
- futuristic AI interfaces
- generic stock-office scenes
- excessive icons
- glossy 3D clichés
- invented performance claims

## 10. Hashtags

Use a small, topic-relevant set. Do not mechanically reuse the same hashtag block on every post.

Include `#OrionEffects` when appropriate and then select hashtags that genuinely match the topic and intended audience.

## 12. Final generation test

Before creating the GitHub issue, ask internally:

**Would a founder or marketing decision-maker learn something about how Orion Effects makes business decisions from this post?**

If not, rewrite it.

Then ask:

**Does the right prospect have a reason to remember the person or company behind this post?**

If not, strengthen the point of view, specificity or commercial consequence.

The goal is not more content.

**The goal is more relevant conversations that can become qualified commercial opportunities.**

# Orion Effects — Content-to-Client Acquisition Strategy

## Objective

The social content system is no longer designed primarily to maximise impressions, followers or publishing volume.

Its commercial objective is:

**Reach → Recognition → Conversation → Qualified Opportunity → Client**

Content supports acquisition. It does not replace prospecting, relationship building or sales.

---

## Primary distribution model

For LinkedIn, prioritise founder/operator-led distribution from Gurmohan Singh's personal profile, supported by the Orion Effects company page as a credibility and proof layer.

The company page should continue publishing selected content, but content should increasingly sound like an experienced operator making a decision rather than an agency teaching generic marketing lessons.

---

## Three mandatory content purposes

Every proposed post must be classified as one of the following before a GitHub review issue is created.

### 1. Authority

Purpose: demonstrate how Orion Effects thinks.

Show a real commercial or advertising decision, diagnostic process, trade-off or operating principle.

Examples:
- Why we would refuse to scale a campaign even when CPL falls
- What we check before increasing budget
- When campaign complexity should be removed
- When the offer is the constraint rather than Meta delivery

### 2. Conversation

Purpose: create relevant discussion with founders, owners, marketing leaders and decision-makers.

Start with a recognisable business situation or a defensible point of view. End with a question only when it genuinely improves the discussion.

The objective is quality of conversation, not comment bait.

### 3. Conversion

Purpose: give a qualified prospect a specific reason to contact Orion Effects.

Use a concrete micro-offer or diagnostic rather than a generic 'contact us' CTA.

Examples:
- Comment `PIPELINE` for the first three areas we would investigate when Meta leads are not becoming sales conversations.
- Message `AUDIT` for the framework we use to decide whether a campaign deserves more budget.

Do not put a conversion CTA on every post.

---

## Preferred writing structure

Use approximately 80% scenario/operator-led content.

**Situation → Observation → What we would check → Commercial decision → What we would change → Business lesson**

Where authentic experience exists, first-person language is preferred:

- `I would...`
- `One of the first things I would check...`
- `I would rather tell a client not to increase spend than...`
- `What would make me scale this?`

Never invent client outcomes, case studies, revenue, ROAS, lead numbers or personal experience.

When a scenario is illustrative, label it clearly as an illustrative scenario.

---

## Commercial measurement

Move discussion beyond platform-only metrics.

Do not stop at:

**CTR → CPC → CPL**

Connect advertising to:

**Spend → Qualified Leads → Conversations → Opportunities → Customers → Revenue**

A post should help a business owner understand a decision, not merely understand an advertising metric.

## AI positioning

Orion Effects is AI-powered but judgement-led.

AI may support:
- research
- analysis
- creative variations
- workflow automation
- reporting
- pattern detection
- repetitive operational work

Human commercial judgement remains responsible for:
- market selection
- offer decisions
- customer value
- acceptable acquisition economics
- budget allocation
- lead qualification
- sales feedback
- stop/scale decisions

Do not imply that AI autonomously makes reliable commercial decisions or guarantees advertising performance.

---

## CTA rules

Do not end every post with a CTA.

Use three CTA modes:

1. **Insight ending** — finish on the commercial lesson. Default for many Authority posts.
2. **Discussion ending** — ask a genuine decision-oriented question. Useful for Conversation posts.
3. **Specific action** — invite a DM/comment around a defined diagnostic or framework. Use selectively for Conversion posts.

Avoid generic support-desk language such as:

- `Facing this issue? Contact us.`
- `We can fix this for you.`
- `Contact us and we will get back to you as soon as possible.`

Do not direct routine social CTAs to the website. Prefer a relevant LinkedIn DM or comment interaction when a CTA is appropriate.

## Creative standard

Normal social posts use static images unless video is explicitly requested.

- Use the real Orion Effects logo without redrawing or distortion.
- Brand palette: deep navy, warm gold and restrained cream.
- Put the key idea directly in the image.
- Prefer premium, realistic, tactile editorial compositions.
- Avoid robots, holograms, fake dashboards, invented statistics, generic stock-office imagery and obvious AI visual clichés.
- Default vertical format for cross-platform creative: 4:5.

The visual should support the commercial idea rather than decorate the post.

## GitHub review issue requirements

Every new content-review issue should include:

- Content purpose: Authority / Conversation / Conversion
- Target decision-maker
- Business situation
- Post hook
- Complete caption
- Commercial decision or lesson
- CTA mode
- Hashtags
- Image key text
- Creative direction
- Human review checklist

### Human review checklist

Before `/approve`, verify:

- Does this sound like an experienced operator rather than a generic marketing teacher?
- Is the target decision-maker identifiable?
- Is there a real business/commercial issue?
- Does the post connect advertising activity to business outcomes?
- Is the point specific enough to remember?
- Is any claimed experience or result authentic?
- Is the CTA appropriate to the content purpose?
- Would the right prospect have a reason to visit the profile, comment or start a conversation?
- Does the creative follow Orion Effects brand standards?

Existing workflow commands remain unchanged:

`/approve` — Ready to Publish  
`/reject` — Revision required  
`/published` — Published and close issue

---

## Acquisition principle

The GitHub content workflow is one component of the acquisition system.

Publishing alone is not the sales strategy.

Content should work alongside targeted prospect identification, thoughtful engagement, direct conversations, follow-up and a clear diagnostic entry offer.

**The goal is not to publish more. The goal is to create more commercially relevant conversations with the right businesses.**

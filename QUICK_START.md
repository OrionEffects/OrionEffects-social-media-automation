# Orion Effects Social Media System — Quick Start

## Purpose

This repository supports a **human-supervised, AI-assisted content-to-client workflow** for Orion Effects.

The objective is not automatic posting or publishing volume.

**Reach → Recognition → Conversation → Qualified Opportunity → Client**

Read first:
1. `CONTENT_ACQUISITION_STRATEGY.md`
2. `CONTENT_GENERATION_STANDARD.md`

## Current workflow

```text
Content Plan / Business Situation
→ AI-Assisted Content Preparation
→ GitHub Content Review Issue
→ Human Review
→ /approve or /reject
→ Ready to Publish
→ Manual Publishing
→ /published
→ Performance Learning
```

Publishing remains human-controlled unless a future integration is explicitly tested and documented as production-ready.

## 1 — Choose the commercial purpose

Every post has one primary purpose:

- **Authority** — demonstrate Orion Effects' advertising and commercial judgement.
- **Conversation** — create useful discussion with founders and decision-makers.
- **Conversion** — give a qualified prospect a specific reason to start a conversation.

Do not create content merely to fill a calendar.

## 2 — Define the target decision-maker

Examples: Founder, CEO, Marketing Director, Growth Lead, Commercial Director or Performance Marketing Lead.

The post should address a business situation that person recognises.

## 3 — Generate operator-led content

Default structure:

**Situation → Observation → What we check → Commercial decision → What changes → Business lesson**

For LinkedIn, first-person operator language is preferred when authentic.

Examples:
- `I would not increase this budget yet.`
- `The CPL went down. I still wouldn't scale it.`
- `One of the first things I would check is what happened after the lead entered the CRM.`

Never fabricate clients, case studies, results, revenue, ROAS, CPL or personal experience. Label hypothetical numerical examples as **illustrative scenarios**.

## 4 — Connect advertising to business outcomes

Move beyond:

**CTR → CPC → CPL**

Toward:

**Spend → Qualified Leads → Conversations → Opportunities → Customers → Revenue**

The content should show the decision behind the metric.

## 5 — Choose the CTA mode

- **Insight** — no hard CTA; finish with the commercial lesson.
- **Discussion** — ask a genuine decision-oriented question.
- **Specific action** — use selectively for Conversion posts.

Example: `If your Meta leads look good in Ads Manager but sales disagrees, message PIPELINE. I'll share the first three places I would investigate.`

Avoid repetitive `contact us`, website CTAs and support-desk language.

## 6 — Prepare the creative

Default: **static 4:5 image** unless video is explicitly requested.

Requirements:
- real Orion Effects logo, unchanged
- deep navy + warm gold + restrained cream
- strongest idea inside the image
- premium, realistic, tactile editorial direction
- one clear focal idea

Avoid robots, holograms, fake dashboards, invented statistics, generic stock-office scenes, futuristic AI interfaces and glossy 3D clichés.

## 7 — Create the GitHub review issue

Title:
`Content Review: Orion Effects — [Post Hook]`

Include:
- Content purpose
- Target decision-maker
- Business situation
- Objective
- Primary platform
- Post hook
- Complete caption
- Commercial decision / lesson
- CTA mode and CTA
- Topic-specific hashtags
- Image key text
- Creative direction
- Human review checklist

Default labels: `content-review`, `review-required`  
Default assignee: `OrionEffects`

## 8 — Human review

Before approving, verify that the post is operator-led, commercially relevant, truthful, tied to business outcomes and appropriate for the target decision-maker.

Commands:

`/approve` — Ready to Publish  
`/reject` — Revision required  
`/published` — Published; close issue

Free-form review comments should not be assumed to trigger automatic AI rewriting unless a tested automation is added later.

## 9 — Publish and distribute

Publishing is not the end of acquisition.

For LinkedIn, prioritise founder/operator-led distribution where appropriate, with the Orion Effects company page supporting credibility.

After publishing, engage with relevant responses, continue useful conversations, identify qualified interest and follow up appropriately. Avoid spammy mass outreach.

## 10 — Measure what matters

Track where possible:
- relevant profile views
- ICP connections
- meaningful conversations
- qualified conversations
- calls booked
- proposals
- clients

Reach and engagement remain useful diagnostic signals, but qualified opportunity creation is the commercial objective.

## Orion Effects positioning

**Meta Ads • Lead Generation • Creative Content • Campaign Strategy**

The higher-level value is better advertising and commercial decision-making.

**What deserves budget → What should be tested → What should stop → What earns the right to scale**

AI supports research, analysis, creative production and operational efficiency. Human judgement remains responsible for commercial decisions.

## Implementation note

Older versions of this guide contained sample automatic Instagram/Facebook publishing code, placeholder API instructions and mock success messages. Those examples are **not the current operating process** and must not be interpreted as verified production integrations.

Current standard:

**AI-assisted preparation + GitHub human review + manual publishing + explicit `/published` confirmation.**

Any future publishing automation must be tested and documented before being described as operational.

## Final test

Before creating a post, ask:

> Would a founder or marketing decision-maker learn something about how Orion Effects makes business decisions from this?

Then:

> Does the right prospect have a reason to remember the person or company behind this post?

If either answer is no, improve the content first.

**The goal is not more posts. The goal is more commercially relevant conversations with the right businesses.**
